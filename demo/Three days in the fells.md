# Three days in the fells

*31 miles · 2,400 m of climbing · one very wet pair of boots*

We left the car at the head of the valley before first light and had the shore to ourselves by six. The water was flat enough to count the ridges twice.

![[dawn-over-the-lake.svg|First light on the lake, 6:10 am]]

## Day one: the long climb

> [!aside|right] The last pull to the top
> ![[summit-portrait.svg]]
> The path climbs steadily for two hours with no shade and one honest stream crossing. **Fill up there**, because there is nothing after it.
>
> Just below the summit the cloud lifted all at once, and the whole ridge came out in one go.
>
> We sat on the cairn for twenty minutes, ate everything in the bag that wasn't dinner, and picked out tomorrow's line across the col. From up there it looked short. It was not short.

![[ridge-at-noon.svg|The ridge at noon]] ![[valley-in-fog.svg|Looking back down the valley]]

## Day two: camp below the col

> [!aside|left] Camp, 1,100 m
